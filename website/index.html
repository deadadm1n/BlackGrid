Community Roles

Ranks And VIP

BlackGrid has simple Discord-facing roles now, with room to grow into server perks and supporter features as AetherReach matures.

Member The basic community role for linked players joining the Discord.
Loyal For regulars who stick around, help the world feel alive, and keep the community moving.
VIP A supporter lane for players who want to back BlackGrid as it grows.
Staff Helpers and moderators keeping AetherReach clean, fair, and stable.
