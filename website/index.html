What It Is

AetherReach is BlackGrid's first public world: modded survival, tech, magic, community trade, and a cleaner rule layer for long-term play.

Server Systems

WatchDog and WatchDog Helper provide Discord linking, rules, Shards, shop access, auctions, and update visibility.

World Rules

Respect players and builds. Chunk Destroyers and heavy mining machines belong in the Mining Dimension. No griefing or stealing.

      How To Read This

      Progression ranks are earned by playtime. Supporter ranks can sit above progression and add higher limits.

      • Claims = FTB Chunks claimed chunks
      • Force Loaded = always-loaded chunks
      • Shards = passive currency income
      • Auctions = active player exchange listings
